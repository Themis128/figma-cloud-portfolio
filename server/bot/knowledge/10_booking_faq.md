# Booking & Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking a Call
Visitors can schedule a teleconference call with Themis directly through this chatbot. If you'd like to book a call, just say something like:
- "I'd like to schedule a call"
- "Can I book a meeting?"
- "I want to arrange a consultation"

The chatbot will help connect you with Themis's booking system.
